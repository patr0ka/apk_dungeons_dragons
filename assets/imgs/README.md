# Ícone do Compêndio

Arte original criada com a ferramenta integrada de geração de imagens.

- `compendio-original.png`: saída original da geração.
- `compendio.png`: PNG de 1024 × 1024 usado no cabeçalho das cinco telas.
- As cópias em `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-*` atendem às densidades do Android. A variante adaptativa usa margens para acomodar as máscaras do launcher.

Direção de arte: livro de consulta aberto, dado de vinte faces e brilho de magia; público de jogadores e mestres que precisam consultar regras rapidamente durante uma sessão. Marfim, dourado e vermelho sobre fundo preto, sem texto.

Prompt de geração:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one original square PNG app icon for a Flutter Dungeons & Dragons reference companion used by players and game masters to quickly look up spells, monsters, classes and equipment in the middle of a tabletop session. Primary visual: a bold open reference spellbook with a single faceted twenty-sided RPG die hovering just above its central fold, integrated with a small clean lightning-like magical spark to suggest instant answers. Strong unified compact emblem, not a scene or a collection of badges. Polished minimal fantasy illustration, broad clean shapes and confident outlines, extremely readable at 48 pixels. Warm ivory book pages, antique gold edges, deep crimson die with subtle warm glow, pure solid black background matching the app. Center the entire emblem inside the central 65 percent of the square so Android circular masking does not crop the design. No words, letters, digits, watermark, UI, mockup, hands or unnecessary ornaments. Full-bleed square background, do not draw a rounded-square frame. Export PNG, ideally 1024x1024. This image will be used directly both as Android launcher icon and as a small logo in every page header.
